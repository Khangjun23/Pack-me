How to use Packing Planner

Packing Planner adds up the weight and volume of everything you pack, tells you whether it all fits, and helps you plan outfits so you don’t bring more than you need.

The usual order is: set up your bags, fill in your inventory, then pack and plan your itinerary, in whichever order suits you.

Before you start

  • Open index.html in your browser. There is nothing to install.
  • The app comes with starter bags and items. Edit or delete them to match your own gear.
  • Every change saves automatically in this browser. Use Export now and then to keep a backup (see Backups).
  • The screen has two sides. The left holds the open suitcase, or the itinerary. The right holds your inventory: one card per item.

1 Set up your bags

Bags sit as tabs above the suitcase. Only one bag is open at a time, and everything you add goes into the open bag.

  1. Click + Add bag. Pick a bag you already have, or click Create a new bag.
  2. Fill in:
    • Name, such as “Carry-on”.
    • Empty weight: what the bag weighs on its own. It counts towards the total.
    • Weight limit: your airline’s limit for this bag.
    • Interior size in cm. The Capacity in litres is worked out for you, and you can type over it.
    • This is a carry-on bag: tick it for cabin bags. Only carry-on bags accept carry-on-only items such as batteries.
  3. Click a bag’s tab to open it. The number on each tab is how many items are in it.
  4. To change a bag, open it and click Edit bag. From there, Remove from trip takes it off this trip only, and Delete everywhere removes it from all trips.

2 Fill in your inventory

The inventory is everything you might bring, shared by all your trips. Click + Add item, or Edit on a card.

Name and short description
Shown on the card. Use the description for a model, size or note.
Category
Pick one or type a new one. Categories become the filter buttons above the cards. Clothes and Shoes are the ones the itinerary checks.
How many you own
The app never lets you pack more than this across the whole trip. Once they are all packed, the card hides. With 7 T-shirts, the card shows “3 of 7 left” as you pack.
Weight (kg)
The weight of one.
Packed volume (L) and dimensions
For rigid or long things like a tripod or laptop, enter the dimensions in cm. The volume is calculated, and the app warns you if the item is too long for a bag. For soft things like clothes, skip the dimensions and type a rough packed volume.
Carry-on only
Tick this for electronics and batteries. These items can only go into a bag marked as carry-on, never a checked bag or a cube.
Icon, emoji or photo
Choose an icon, or type or paste any emoji in “Or use any emoji”. On a Mac, press Control-Command-Space or fn-E to open the emoji keyboard. You can also add a photo, which replaces the icon. Photos are shrunk to save space.

Copy on a card makes a duplicate with the same weight and size. Rename it for things like a second pair of similar trousers. Delete item is in the edit window, and it also removes the item from any bags and outfits.

Use the search box and category buttons to find items quickly.

3 Packing cubes

A packing cube is an item that holds other items, so you can fill each cube and then put the cube in a bag.

  1. Add an item and tick This is a packing cube that holds other items. Enter how much it holds in litres and its own weight.
  2. Cubes appear as tabs in the Cubes row under the bag tabs. Click one to fill it. The suitcase becomes the open cube.
  3. Add clothes and other items to it, just as you would to a bag.
  4. Open a bag again, then drag or add the cube’s card into it. The cube brings its contents along, and their weight and volume count towards the bag.

A cube can’t go inside another cube, and carry-on-only items can’t go in a cube. Taking a cube out of a bag keeps its contents inside the cube. The top bar warns you if a filled cube isn’t in any bag yet.

4 Pack first, or plan first

Use the Packing and Itinerary switch at the top left. Both pages use the same inventory, so you can start with either.

Pack, then plan

Pack what you think you need. Then plan each day’s outfit, and the Outfit check tells you what you packed but won’t wear, or what you are short of.

Plan, then pack

Plan outfits day by day first, and the Outfit check tells you how many of each item to pack. Then switch to Packing and pack exactly that.

5 Packing

Adding and removing items

  • Drag a card by its picture onto the open suitcase, or click its Add button. Adding the same item again raises its count, such as ×3.
  • To take something out, drag its box out of the suitcase, or click the × on the box. Each removal takes out one.
  • Cards show In bag ×2 for what is in the open bag or cube, and how many you have left.
  • The app refuses an item with a short message when it doesn’t belong. For example, a camera in a checked bag, a cube inside a cube, or more items than you own.

Reading the suitcase

  • Weight and Volume meters show the open bag’s totals against its limits, including the bag’s own weight. They turn amber at 90% and red when over.
  • The status line under the meters gives the reason, such as “Over by 3.5 L” or “Tripod is too long for this bag”.
  • Each packed item is a box. Box size shows volume, so bigger boxes take more space. Colour shows weight: darker means heavier. Hover over a box for its exact numbers.
  • The Trip total in the top bar adds up every bag on the trip.

Make room

When a bag is near or over a limit, Make room appears above the suitcase. It lists the heaviest and the bulkiest items, with each one’s share of the total. It also tells you which single item would fix the problem if removed. Click Remove beside one to take it out.

6 Itinerary

Dates and flights

  • Set Fly out and Come back under Travel dates. One card appears for each day of the trip, up to 60 days.
  • Under Flights, fill in your outbound and return flights: number, from, to, times and booking reference. Each flight shows on the day it departs and lands.
  • Type Where you will be on each day.

Outfits

  • Click a day to select it, then use Add on a card. You can also drag a card straight onto a day.
  • On the itinerary, cards show Planned ×N, which is how many of that item your outfits need.
  • Each item in an outfit shows a tag:
    • Re-wear: a button for items you own several of. Turn it on to wear yesterday’s piece again instead of a fresh one, so there’s one less to pack.
    • Re-worn: for items you own only one of, worn again.
    • Washed: a piece that came back clean from laundry, so there’s nothing more to pack.
  • The re-wear counter, such as Re-wear 2×, shows how many times that piece has been re-worn since it was fresh or last washed.

Laundry days

  • Click Laundry on a day when you will wash clothes. Everything worn so far is washed at the end of that day and can be worn again as a fresh piece.
  • All dirty items are ticked by default. Untick anything you won’t wash.
  • After a wash, you can wear a washed piece for several days in a row by turning on Re-wear on the days after.

Outfit check

The Outfit check compares your outfits with what is packed across all bags and cubes:

  • Pack N more: your outfits need more than you’ve packed.
  • N more packed than planned: you could leave some at home.
  • Planned N, but you own M: plan a re-wear or a laundry day.
  • Packed to match: all good.
  • It also lists how many days each item is worn and how many times it’s re-worn, how many items laundry saves you, and any clothes or shoes you packed but didn’t put in an outfit.

7 Trips

Use the Trip menu at the top to switch between trips. Each trip has its own bags, packing and itinerary, and all trips share one inventory.

  • New starts an empty trip.
  • Rename changes the trip’s name.
  • Duplicate copies the whole trip, which is handy for a similar trip.
  • Delete removes the trip, but your items and bags stay.

8 Checklist for Apple Notes

  1. Click Checklist in the top bar. It lists everything packed, by bag, with each cube’s contents grouped under it.
  2. Click Download for Notes, then in Notes on your Mac choose File › Import Markdown. This needs macOS 26 or later.
  3. Or click Copy as text, paste it into a note, select it, and click the Checklist button in Notes.

The note syncs to your iPhone through iCloud, so you can tick things off as you pack.

9 Themes

Choose a colour from Theme in the top bar: Automatic (follows your device’s light or dark mode), Sky blue, Dark blue, Sage green, Wine red, Black or White.

10 Backups and other devices

  • Your data lives in this browser on this device only. Clearing your browser’s site data erases it.
  • Export downloads everything, including items, bags, trips and photos, as one file. Keep it somewhere safe.
  • Import loads an exported file and replaces what is in the app now.
  • To move to another device or browser, Export on one and Import on the other. The two copies don’t stay in sync afterwards.
  • If storage fills up, the app says so. Remove some photos to free space.

11 On a phone

  • On a narrow screen, the page stacks: suitcase or itinerary first, then the inventory.
  • The Add button is the quickest way to pack on a phone.
  • To drag, hold a card by its picture and move towards the top edge of the screen. The page scrolls up until the suitcase or day appears, and then you can drop. Swiping on a card’s text scrolls the page normally.
  • In Safari, use Share › Add to Home Screen to give the app its own icon.

12 Tips and shortcuts

  • Press Esc while dragging to cancel and send the item back.
  • Everything works with the keyboard: Tab to a button and press Enter.
  • Hover over packed boxes and outfit tags to see more detail.
  • Starter weights and sizes are estimates. Weigh your own gear for the best results.
